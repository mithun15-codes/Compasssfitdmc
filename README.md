# Compass SFI TDMC — TD Medical College, Alappuzha

> *A website project built for a friend.*

**Live Website:** [https://compasssfitdmc.in/](https://compasssfitdmc.in/)

## About the Website

**Compass SFI TDMC** is a comprehensive student and visitor guide to **Government T.D. Medical College, Alappuzha** (*"Where Medicine Meets Compassion"*). Designed as a one-stop digital companion for incoming students, current medico community members, and visitors, the website showcases campus life, academic departments, residential facilities, and the surrounding heritage of Alappuzha.

### Key Sections

- **Campus Tour (`/pages/campus/`)** — Overview of key campus facilities and student spaces, including the Central Library, Auditorium, Cafeteria, Sports Complex, and Wellness Center.
- **Hostel Life (`/pages/hostel/`)** — Information and guides on student residential facilities across Men's Hostels (MH1, MH2, MH3) and Women's Hostels (WH1, WH2).
- **Academics (`/pages/academics/`)** — Details on foundational medical departments and programmes, including Anatomy, Physiology, and Biochemistry.
- **Explore Alappuzha (`/pages/explore/`)** — A curated local guide to *"The Venice of the East"*, featuring nearby places to visit, temples, churches, mosques, and miscellaneous essentials around Vandanam and Alappuzha.
- **Contact (`/pages/contact.html`)** — Location details, contact numbers, and links to reach out to the college and student community.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & CSS3** (Custom responsive styling with modular CSS for layout, navigation, and footer)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Interactive navigation, scroll animations, and dynamic counters)
- **Font Awesome & Google Fonts** (`Playfair Display` and `Inter`)
